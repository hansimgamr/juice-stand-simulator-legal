Support

Juice Stand Simulator is an offline, single-player iPhone and iPad game. No account is required.

Updated September 23, 2026 for version 1.0.0 (5).

Quick help

  • Open How to Play from the planning screen for the complete gameplay guide.
  • Open Settings for haptics, reduced motion, timer assistance, and left-handed layout. All game sounds are currently disabled.
  • Use Share Diagnostics in Settings to create a privacy-safe text report if something isn't working, then share it through the system share sheet to whoever you want to help you.
  • Use Reset Campaign only when you want to replace current campaign progress with a clean first morning.

Planning and serving

  • Check the forecast above Menu, Market, Store, and Open. Choose drinks on Menu, buy supplies on Market, and review pantry inventory on Store.
  • Swipe the Market bag down to collapse it, or use its pull handle to expand it.
  • Tap Open for the day to begin. If conditions are not met, review the warning before choosing whether to start anyway.
  • Read each customer's ticket: fill levels and ingredient amounts vary. Tap the illustrated tools to make the drink, use Undo to correct it, then give it to the customer.
  • Some customers are in a hurry. Their shorter timer comes with a displayed bonus for successful service. Timer assistance remains available in Settings.
  • Review the day's line-item Receipts after service.

TestFlight help

If an update is missing, reopen TestFlight and check the app's available builds. Include the version and build you see when reporting the issue. Beta availability depends on Apple's processing and review as well as tester assignment. You can send beta feedback through TestFlight.

Reporting a problem

Contact hansimgamr@gmail.com for help. You may attach the optional in-app Share diagnostics report. Include:

  • the app version and build number;
  • the device model and iOS or iPadOS version;
  • the game mode, campaign day, and what happened immediately before the issue.

Do not send passwords or unrelated personal information. Juice Stand Simulator has no account and support will never ask for one.
