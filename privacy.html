Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Juice Stand Simulator does not automatically collect or transmit personal data to the developer. The game has no advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics service, and the developer does not sell personal data.

The game does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, social features, cloud synchronization, or in-app purchases. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on the device. A player may choose to export diagnostic information using the system share sheet; the player controls whether and where that file is sent.

Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to the device's normal backup behavior. The in-game reset control can replace current campaign progress without deleting the app.

Optional diagnostics and support

Share diagnostics creates a text report about the app version, game state, and save recovery. Nothing is sent automatically. Review the report before using the system share sheet; you choose the recipient and sharing service. If you send a report or feedback to the developer, that information is used to investigate and respond to your request. Do not include passwords or unrelated personal information.

Apple services and this website

TestFlight distribution, beta feedback, and Apple-managed diagnostics are handled by Apple under its own policies and your device settings. Device backups may include local game data according to your backup settings. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitor information to operate the hosting service. These services are separate from the offline game.

Apple privacy information · GitHub privacy statement

Juice Stand Simulator is not intended to collect information from children or adults. If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before that version is released.
